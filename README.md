## ⚡ The Simple 3-Step Hustle Loop
1. **Step 1: Work (Craft Shoe)**: Hammer and stitch crepe sole leather shoes at Mazi Okonkwo's bench (`+$5,500 Cash • +25 XP`).
2. **Step 2: Restock (Ariaria Wholesale)**: Buy bulk shoe inventory (burnished loafers, mules, slides) from Ariaria Market factories via Driver Chidi's Keke.
3. **Step 3: Sell (Retail Walk-in Customer)**: Sell footwear to eager street shoppers like Customer Ada directly from Stall #14B.

---

## 🎭 Animated Characters
- **Mazi Okonkwo**: Master Cobbler hammering rhythmically at his wooden workbench.
- **Player Character**: Articulated avatar at Stall #14B with custom attire and working animations.
- **Customer Ada**: Walking civilian buyer with dynamic shopping speech bubbles.
- **Driver Chidi**: Bouncing yellow Nigerian Keke Napep tricycle with rotating wheels and loaded shoe cartons.
- **Mama Chinyere**: Street vendor in traditional gele stirring a steaming pot of spicy jollof rice.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend / Microservices**: FastAPI, Pydantic, Python 3.12, PostgreSQL 16.
